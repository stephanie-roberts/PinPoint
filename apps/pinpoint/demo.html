Four steps to your first snag

No account to set up for clients. They sign in with a code sent to their email.

  1. 01

    Switch it on

    Open /demo.html?feedback to turn the widget on.

  2. 02

    Sign in

    Add your name and email, then type the 6-digit code.

  3. 03

    Draw a box

    Click the violet button in the corner, then drag a box around anything.

  4. 04

    Send it

    Pick a type, write a note and send. It shows up in the inbox straight away.
