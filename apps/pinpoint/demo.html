Try PinPoint

This page is set up as the "test" site, so anything you pin here lands in your inbox.

  1. 01

    Open /demo.html?feedback to switch the widget on.

  2. 02

    Sign in with your name and email, then type the 6-digit code.

  3. 03

    Click the feedback button, choose the pencil and drag a box around anything.

  4. 04

    Pick a type, write a note and send. It shows up in the inbox straight away.
